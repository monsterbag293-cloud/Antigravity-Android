    autonumber
    actor User as User / App Setup
    participant Downloader as Android OkHttpClient Downloader

    participant FS as App Internal Storage (/rootfs/usr/bin/)

    Downloader->>GCS: GET /antigravity-hub/2.29.1-5241471524274176/...
    Downloader->>FS: Stream bytes to .language_server.partial.<pid>
    Note over Downloader: Computes SHA-256 and CRC32C<br>concurrently while streaming

    Downloader->>Downloader: Validate CRC32C matches 'gCBY0w=='
    Downloader->>FS: chmod 755 .language_server.partial.<pid>
    Downloader->>FS: Atomic rename: mv .partial -> /rootfs/usr/bin/language_server
    Downloader-->>User: Setup complete; ready to launch

### 2.1 Security Boundary Audit
- **Application UID**: `10385` (`u0_a385`). The process runs completely without root privileges.
- **SELinux Domain**: `u:r:untrusted_app_34`. This is the standard, unprivileged third-party application sandbox.
- **Root Status**: No root privileges requested or required (`id: uid=10385(u0_a385) gid=10385(u0_a385)`).
- **SELinux AVC Denials**: Audited during execution (`adb logcat -d | grep -i "avc: denied.*u0_a385"`):
  - **Zero** ptrace denials.
  - **Zero** execution denials.
  - **Zero** socket bind denials.
